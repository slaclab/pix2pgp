ASIC/FPGA Top-Level Port Description

This section describes every generic and port of the two top-level entities a user instantiates:

  • ASIC Top-Level Wrapper — the per-ASIC wrapper Pix2Pgp<AsicName>Top that goes into the ASIC’s digital section (one instance per Pix2PGP core / lane)

  • FPGA Top-Level (Pix2PgpAsicStreamRx) — Pix2PgpAsicStreamRx, the FPGA receiver that consolidates every lane of one ASIC

For step-by-step integration guides, see How to Integrate the Pix2PGP Core in Your ASIC and How to Integrate the Pix2PGP FPGA Receiver in Your FPGA.
